We trade our own capital and build our own systems.

Pal Quant Labs is a proprietary trading and technology firm. We invest our own money across equities, derivatives, commodities and currencies — and engineer the research and software behind every position.

We do not manage client funds.

About

We run two connected practices. One deploys capital in markets; the other builds the models and software that support it. Because we answer only to ourselves, we work on a research horizon rather than a reporting cycle.

Own Capital
Every position is funded internally and held on our own book.
Built In-House
Models and systems are designed and run by the same team that trades them.

Capital

We manage a single book, allocated by model and bounded by limits set in advance. Research decides what we hold; discipline decides how long we hold it.

Systematic Allocation
Nothing is traded on a model that has not been researched and tested first.
Risk & Hedging
Hedges and loss limits are set before a position opens, not after it moves.

Technology

We design and operate the software behind our work — the platforms, interfaces, analytics and modelling tools our own trading runs on. It is infrastructure written by the people who have to trade on it, which is a different standard than shipping it for someone else to.

Platforms & Systems
Software, interfaces and algorithmic systems we build and maintain ourselves.
Analytics & AI
Machine-learning and financial modelling tools, built and tested against our own book.
